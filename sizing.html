Portfolio construction over directional trading

Sep 2023

Directional trading is highly overrated, while portfolio construction is incredibly underrated. The same picks, but with different weights or hedges or rebalance frequencies can beget a wider range of outcomes than finding new winners.

Don't just look further... dig deeper.
The psychological allure of getting a “trade” right is very exciting !
Performance dispersion comes from “entry”, “exit” and “sizing”
Instead of focussing too much on markets, focus more on money management and overall structure of the portfolio.

The problem with "information" is our reading of it. Regardless of it being true or false, we attempt to conform to what we already assume to be true, and distrust that which we went in believing to be false. The goal, then, is not to seek information, but to clear the mind.

One can ask questions either in distress (help!) or drive for $ (excited!) or genuine curiosity.
To all who ask:
"You're never going to learn something as profoundly as when it's purely out of curiosity"
-Christopher Nolan

Stack size, variance and risk of ruin

Sep 2023

Stack size can influence outcomes. If your stack is too small, routine variance can

bankrupt you, even if you have an edge. Retail traders with tiny accounts and undercapitalized poker players face extreme path dependence, need good luck at the outset, and face high risk of ruin.

• Good players have an edge, and the game involves skill, but there is so much variance that in the short run it’s often difficult to distinguish luck from skill on short time horizons. The way traders and poker players deal with the psychology of drawdown and variance is a major determinant of their long-term success.
